Questions

Do my clients need an account?

No. Each client gets a private link. Anyone with the link can fill in that page, so send it only to them.

Is a typed signature valid?

A typed name with a timestamp and a record of exactly what was agreed is a simple electronic signature, which is accepted for many everyday agreements. For high-value or regulated contracts, check with a solicitor.

Where is my data kept?

On Amazon Web Services, encrypted at rest. Deleting a client removes their answers, files and messages.

Can I use my own booking tool?

Yes. Paste your Calendly, Cal.com or Google Calendar booking link and clients are sent there.
